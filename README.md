## 🎮 About the Game

WheelMates is a cooperative adventure game developed and published by Firevolt. It released on September 1, 2026. Two players control tiny RC cars through an eccentric professor’s oversized home laboratory, solving puzzles and navigating platforming challenges.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **WheelMates**. This trainer provides optional utilities for WheelMates’ two-player RC-car adventure, including enhanced vehicle control, safer traversal, convenient repositioning, and flexible puzzle exploration.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed WheelMates experience, need help with difficult RC-car traversal, or want to explore the laboratory and its secrets without repeated failed attempts.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

WheelMates is built around driving tiny RC cars through oversized rooms. Use the trainer’s movement options to make steering more manageable, recover from awkward positions, and reduce setbacks during ramps, platforming routes, rope swings, and magnetic-wheel sections.

### Tools & Inventory

WheelMates does not use a traditional equipment or loot inventory. Progress centers on environmental interaction, puzzle completion, exploration, and collecting memory cards that contribute to the journal.

### World & Environment

Explore the professor’s home laboratory room by room. Search shelves, desks, work areas, and other household locations for hidden routes, interactive objects, secrets, and journal-related discoveries. Cooperative coordination is required throughout the adventure.

### Combat & Weapons

WheelMates has no combat system. The trainer therefore focuses on driving, traversal, exploration, puzzle assistance, and recovery from difficult environmental sections.
